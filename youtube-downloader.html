How to Download YouTube Videos for Free in 2026

YouTube is the world's largest video platform with over 800 million videos. People want to download YouTube videos for many reasons — watching offline during travel, saving educational content, keeping copies of videos that might be deleted, or extracting audio for music.

In 2026, the best method for most users is cobalt.tools — a free, open source, no-ads downloader that works directly in your browser without any installation. Simply open cobalt.tools, paste your YouTube URL, choose your quality, and download.

Step by Step Guide — How to Download YouTube Videos

  • Copy the YouTube video URL from your browser or the YouTube app Share button
  • Open cobalt.tools in your browser
  • Paste the URL into the input field on cobalt.tools
  • Select your preferred video quality (4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p or MP3 audio)
  • Click Download and save the file to your device

How to Download YouTube Videos on Android

Open Chrome on your Android phone and visit cobalt.tools. Paste the YouTube link and tap Download. The video will save to your Downloads folder. You can also use the yt-dlp tool via Termux if you are a more advanced user who wants higher quality options and playlist downloads.

How to Download YouTube Videos on iPhone

Open cobalt.tools in Safari on your iPhone. Paste the YouTube URL and tap Download. When the download prompt appears, tap Options and select Save to Files. You can then move the video to your Photos app using the Files app.

How to Download YouTube Videos as MP3

All three tools listed above support YouTube to MP3 conversion. On cobalt.tools, select the Audio Only option before downloading. This extracts just the audio track from the video and saves it as an MP3 file — perfect for music, podcasts, or lectures.

YouTube Premium vs Downloading

YouTube Premium offers an official offline download feature that is fully legal and integrated into the YouTube app. If you regularly watch YouTube offline, YouTube Premium is the safest and most convenient option. Third party downloading tools are best for occasional use.
