Privacy Policy — ArcComic

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how the ArcComic automation tool ("the App") handles data when connecting to the Pinterest API.

What the App does

ArcComic is a personal automation tool that reads publicly posted comic artwork and captions from our own Telegram channel (t.me/ArcComic) and creates Pins on our own connected Pinterest account(s) to share that content with a wider audience.

What data the App accesses

The App only creates new Pins on the Pinterest account(s) we explicitly connect it to. It does not read, collect, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user, board, or account. It does not access personal information of Pinterest visitors, followers, or anyone who views or interacts with our Pins.

What data the App stores

The App stores, locally on our own device, only the information needed to operate: API access tokens for our own connected accounts, and the comic metadata (title/code, artist name, category, and an image link) needed to build each Pin. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling purposes.

Data retention

Posted-item records are kept only to prevent duplicate posting and for our own basic usage statistics (e.g. how many Pins were posted). This data is stored locally and is not transmitted to any third party beyond the Pinterest API itself, which is required to create the Pins.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the App can be sent via our Telegram channel: t.me/ArcComic.
